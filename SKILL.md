## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one small, isolated typography-as-image landmark. The memorable subject becomes lines, frames, columns, blocks and a few structural nodes, and the lettering is part of that structure rather than a caption added afterwards. Keep the subject small in warm-white negative space. Colour roles stay distinct: orange for structure and the type skeleton, cyan for secondary information, yellow for lift, magenta as a small jump, black for weight. It is not a full-bleed layout, an equal multi-focus arrangement, ordinary headline-plus-body typesetting, a realistic illustration, a cartoon, 3D, cheap colour clash or a template poster.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-222/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
